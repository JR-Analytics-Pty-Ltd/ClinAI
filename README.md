ClinAI

A clinician-led platform for reproducible healthcare AI research

Developed by JR Analytics Pty Ltd | Adelaide, Australia

ClinAI is an AWS-hosted clinical AI research platform designed to support the development, evaluation and validation of artificial intelligence models in healthcare.

Our aim is to bridge clinical expertise and AI development, making healthcare AI research more accessible, structured, reproducible and clinically relevant.

What ClinAI supports

Clinical AI research: Development and evaluation of models addressing meaningful clinical problems.

Reproducible workflows: Structured documentation of datasets, methods, code, experiments and results.

Cloud-based research infrastructure: AWS computing, data storage and analytical environments.

Model evaluation: Assessment of predictive performance, clinical relevance and limitations.

Research collaboration: Opportunities for clinicians, researchers and technical teams to work together on clinical AI projects.

Current research areas

Sepsis and critical care

Reproduction and evaluation of published sepsis research using MIMIC-IV data, with a focus on organ dysfunction, clinical trajectories and outcomes.

Emergency department triage

Investigation of AI-assisted emergency triage, including classification performance and clinically important misclassification.

ICU admission prediction

Research into predicting ICU admission following emergency department presentation using early clinical observations and patient trajectories.

These projects are at different stages of development and validation. Research findings should not be interpreted as clinically validated decision-support tools.

Research infrastructure

ClinAI uses Amazon Web Services (AWS), including cloud computing, research data storage and database infrastructure.

Research workflows are organised around individual projects, with structured documentation to support reproducibility and evaluation.
